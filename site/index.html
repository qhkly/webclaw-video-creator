Core capabilities

An editing suite wired for AI — and you stay the editor.

AI Director

The app exposes its editing tools to an AI coding agent you already run — Claude Code or Codex on your machine, or a ChatGPT session connected through an OpenAI MCP tunnel. The agent drafts scripts, adjusts scenes, and triggers voiceover or image generation for you. It gets only the video tools, never a shell, and every write waits for your approval.

Text-based cutting

Import a recorded talking-head or demo video, transcribe it, then cut by deleting words and pauses in the transcript. The preview skips removed ranges in real time; the export is a clean FFmpeg cut with short audio fades at every seam.

Scene workflow

Scripts split into scenes on blank lines. Each scene gets a template, narration, a duration, and props — managed as cards, played back through an in-app Remotion preview.

AI voiceover

Narration is synthesized with Edge TTS voices, previewed per scene, and mixed into the final render. A voice-cloning integration point is reserved for later.

AI images, your account

Scene images are generated with GPT Image through your own ChatGPT/Codex login — no API key to buy — and auto-cropped to 16:9, 9:16, or 1:1 for the scene.

Local projects, standard formats

Projects, media, and rendered files stay on your disk. Export is H.264/AAC MP4 via Remotion and FFmpeg — no lock-in, no cloud library to migrate out of.

FAQ

Questions people ask first.

Do I need my own AI accounts?

Yes. The AI features drive agents and accounts you already have: a local Claude Code or Codex install, or your ChatGPT login. Model usage is billed by your provider to your own account — Video Creator does not include or resell AI quota.

Can it generate video clips with AI?

Not today, and we would rather say so. Video Creator assembles videos from scene templates, generated images, imported footage, text, and voiceover. AI-generated video clips are on the exploratory roadmap, not in the product.

What does it export?

Standard H.264/AAC MP4 files rendered through Remotion and FFmpeg. The text-based cutter likewise exports MP4. Everything lands in a folder you choose, on your own disk.

What are the system requirements?

macOS, Windows, or Linux. The rendering pipeline currently runs through a local Node.js sidecar, so Node.js 18+ must be installed; the installers are becoming self-contained. Transcription options work best with an OpenAI-compatible API key or a local whisper.cpp model, and always fall back to silence detection.

How does licensing work?

The free tier needs no purchase: AI Director, AI cleanup, image generation through your own connected AI accounts, basic editing, and 720p watermarked export are available for personal use. A Pro subscription — monthly or yearly in the WebClaw Store — unlocks up to 4K export without a watermark plus a commercial-use license. Your projects and exported videos always stay on your disk.

Where do my projects and footage live?

On your machine. Projects, media, and renders are local files. AI features send only what you approve — a prompt, a script section — directly to your own provider account.

Is it open source?

No. WebClaw Video Creator is a proprietary desktop app. Prebuilt installers are published on GitHub Releases, and issues can be reported there.
